The Smarter Way

Less wasted time. More marks.

02

Fix weak spots, skip the rest

See which topics are costing you marks. Double down there instead of re-reading chapters you've already got.

04

06

Stay accountable with peers

Study rooms keep you consistent. Share problems, compare approaches, and keep the pace when motivation dips.
